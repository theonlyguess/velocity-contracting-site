Privacy notice

How this website handles information when you contact Velocity Contracting LLC.

Velocity Contracting LLC operates this website to describe our facilities contracting services and to make it easy to reach our Denver office.

What we collect

We do not operate an account system or a contact database on this static website. If you use the quote form, the message is emailed to guess@velocitycontractingllc.com through Formsubmit. It is not stored on the GitHub Pages host. If you email or call us directly, we receive whatever information you choose to include.

  • Name, organization, email, phone, interest, and message from the quote form
  • Email content you send to guess@velocitycontractingllc.com
  • Phone conversations and voicemail when you call 806-252-7815
  • Ordinary web-server or hosting logs that may include IP address, browser type, and pages requested (standard for hosting providers)

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run advertising pixels or third-party marketing trackers on this site.
  • We do not use analytics packages beyond whatever your hosting provider may log by default.

How we use contact information

We use messages and calls to respond to quote requests, solicitations, insurance or COI questions, and partnership inquiries related to Velocity Contracting LLC.

Cookies and local storage

This site may use a service worker and browser cache so the pages load reliably and can be added to a phone home screen. That storage stays on your device for site files. It is not used to track you across other websites.

External links

References to Colorado Secretary of State business search or BidNet are informational. Those sites have their own privacy practices.
