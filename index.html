About

A Colorado LLC built for facilities work that has to hold up under scrutiny.

Velocity Contracting LLC is a Denver company formed to pursue and perform facility-services contracts for public agencies and commercial owners. Field work is delivered through subcontractors; our role is prime management — bidding, contracting, compliance, and quality oversight.

We keep the model honest: no inflated claims, no self-perform theater. Buyers get one accountable contractor and a network of specialists who actually do the work.
