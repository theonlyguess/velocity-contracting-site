Facilities

Facilities covers janitorial, grounds, waste, snow, pest, mechanical, fire and life safety, pavement, and on-call work. The company's focus is government contracts.

Janitorial, custodial & floor care

NAICS 561720

Day porter and night custodial programs sized to occupancy, traffic, and inspection standards. Restroom care, trash and recycling, hard-floor and carpet maintenance, high-touch disinfection, and project cleans for turnovers or events.

What we deliver

  • Scheduled daily / weekly cleaning
  • Restroom & breakroom programs
  • Floor care & carpet extraction
  • Supply restocking as specified
  • Post-construction & turnover cleans

Typical sites

  • Municipal & county buildings
  • Schools & campus spaces
  • Offices & civic centers
  • Libraries & recreation facilities

How we contract it

  • Fixed monthly or unit-priced
  • Scope sheets tied to floor plans
  • Quality checklists & site walks
  • Prime accountability for performance

  • Turf mowing, edging & trimming

  • Leaf, brush & grounds debris cleanup

Typical sites

  • Storm / extra-work rates defined
  • Photo documentation on request

On-call & mobile field response

Cross-cutting capability across facility trades

When something cannot wait for the next scheduled visit, Velocity provides on-call and mobile field response: a single point of contact to dispatch the right crew for urgent cleanups, on-call debris, spill response, access issues, storm aftermath, and other time-sensitive facility needs. Coordination is mobile-first. Site contacts reach us by phone or email, and we mobilize field crews and trade partners under our contract.

What we deliver

  • Defined on-call windows
  • Urgent facility call-outs
  • On-call debris and spill response
  • After-hours / weekend mobilizations
  • Photo confirmation of completed work

Typical sites

  • Multi-building agency campuses
  • Sites with public foot traffic
  • Portfolios without full-time FM staff
  • Locations needing one vendor number

How we contract it

  • Retainer + per-call rates
  • Escalation contacts on file
  • Response-time targets by priority
  • Works alongside scheduled trades
