Building repair and construction

NAICS 238210 · 238220 · 238330 · 238350 · 238990

Velocity primes building repair and construction as a general contractor. The work is the repair and construction of buildings: electrical, plumbing, heating and gas, interior doors, flooring, and stair or deck repair, coordinated with the facility services already on this page. Field crews and licensed trade partners execute under our contract. Permits are pulled when the jurisdiction requires them. This is building work. It is not heavy civil, highway, or bridge work.

What we deliver

  • Electrical repair
  • Plumbing repair
  • Heating and gas repair
  • Interior doors
  • Flooring
  • Stair or deck repair

Typical sites

  • Homes and occupied residences
  • Commercial and multi-building sites
  • Municipal and county buildings
  • Schools and campus spaces

How we contract it

  • Prime agreement with the owner or buyer
  • Itemized repair scopes
  • Licensed trades when a license is required
  • Written changes before extra work proceeds
