Facility services for Colorado properties

Velocity bids, manages, and delivers multi-trade facility work. Field crews and licensed trade partners execute under our contracts; we remain the accountable prime. Additional facility trades can be scoped as solicitations require.

Janitorial & custodial

NAICS 561720

Day porter and night custodial programs sized to occupancy, traffic, and inspection standards. Restroom care, trash/recycling, hard-floor and carpet maintenance, high-touch disinfection, and project cleans for turnovers or events.

What we deliver

  • Scheduled daily / weekly cleaning
  • Restroom & breakroom programs
  • Floor care & carpet extraction
  • Supply restocking as specified
  • Post-construction & turnover cleans

Typical sites

  • Municipal & county buildings
  • Schools & campus spaces
  • Offices & civic centers
  • Libraries & recreation facilities

How we contract it

  • Fixed monthly or unit-priced
  • Scope sheets tied to floor plans
  • Quality checklists & site walks
  • Prime accountability for performance

Landscaping & grounds

What we deliver

  • Turf mowing & edging
  • Bed maintenance & mulch
  • Seasonal planting cycles
  • Irrigation checks & adjustments
  • Leaf & debris cleanup

Typical sites

  • Civic campuses & parks facilities
  • School grounds
  • Office & industrial parks

  • Photo documentation on request

On-call & mobile field response

Cross-cutting capability across facility trades

When something cannot wait for the next scheduled visit, Velocity provides on-call and mobile field response: a single point of contact to dispatch the right crew for urgent cleanups, spill response, access issues, storm aftermath, and other time-sensitive facility needs. Coordination is mobile-first: site contacts reach us by phone or email, and we mobilize field crews and trade partners under our contract.

What we deliver

  • Defined on-call windows
  • Rapid cleanup & spill response
  • After-hours / weekend mobilizations
  • Photo confirmation of completed work
  • Follow-on scheduled service if needed

Typical sites

  • Multi-building agency campuses
  • Sites with public foot traffic
  • Portfolios without full-time FM staff
  • Locations needing one vendor number

How we contract it

  • Retainer + per-call rates
  • Escalation contacts on file
  • Response-time targets by priority
  • Works alongside scheduled trades

Public-sector contracting

What we deliver

  • Single point of contact

  • Performance documentation
  • Renewal-ready records

Typical buyers

  • Special districts
  • School districts & higher ed
  • State & quasi-public entities

How we contract it

  • Prime agreement with agency
  • Flow-down to field crews / partners
  • Room for additional facility trades
