Government contracts are the focus.

We work in four groups: facilities, traffic control, software and ticketing, and residential building. Residential building is done in-house, and it is a side, not the main line. Commercial and government construction is subcontracted to other builders.

Company

Who we are

Velocity Contracting LLC bids, manages, and delivers that work from Denver. Field crews and licensed trade partners execute under our contracts. We remain the accountable prime for schedule, quality, compliance, and closeout documentation.

Buyers hold one contractor responsible while drawing on qualified specialists for each facilities trade: janitorial, grounds, waste, pest, snow, windows, building maintenance, mechanical, fire and life safety, electrical, pavement support, mobile response, and related scopes as solicitations require.

We serve the Front Range as our primary geography, with statewide capacity for public contracts. Our office is in Denver at 5201 Helena Street.

Operating model

Accountable prime. Qualified crews and partners.

How work moves from solicitation to closeout.

  • We own the contract relationship

    Velocity is the prime. Buyers have one point of contact for scope, schedule, invoicing, insurance certificates, and performance issues.

  • Field work is staffed for the trade

    Site work is performed by field crews and licensed trade partners matched to each scope. Qualification, insurance flow-down, and quality expectations sit in our agreements with them.

  • Documentation stays with the file

    Commercial insurance and certificates of insurance are issued to awarding agencies as required by contract. Coverage program includes CGL, hired/non-owned auto, umbrella, and workers’ compensation as specified in each solicitation.

  • Colorado good standing

    We maintain Colorado LLC Good Standing so agency vendors and insurers can confirm a legitimate, active entity, not a shell brochure.
